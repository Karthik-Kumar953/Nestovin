# Nestovin image shot list (Resident and Worker apps)

Generate these and save them in `docs/Design/images/` with the exact file names below. I pick them up from there.

- Photos: JPG or PNG, at least the size listed.
- Illustrations: PNG with a **transparent background**, 1024 × 1024.
- No text, logos or watermarks inside the images; I add the logo and copy in the design.
- Regenerate anything that shows distorted hands or faces, or extra fingers.

## 1. Style prefixes (paste before every prompt)

**Photo prefix**
> Photorealistic lifestyle photograph, warm natural morning daylight, modern Indian urban apartment in a gated community, clean and lived-in, not luxury. Soft depth of field, calm and premium mood, colour palette of white, warm wood, soft mint and teal (#00897B) accents. Domestic staff wear a neat teal polo shirt with a small white house-shaped emblem on the chest, dark trousers, hair tied back, friendly and professional. Respectful, dignified, candid, not posed stock photography. No text, no watermark.

**Illustration prefix**
> Soft 3D clay-style illustration, single object centred, rounded friendly shapes, matte finish, gentle studio lighting with a soft shadow below. Limited palette: teal #00897B, deep teal #00695C, fresh green #43A047, mint #E6F4F1, white, a touch of warm amber #F59E0B for highlights. Minimal detail, premium and calm. Transparent background, no text.
