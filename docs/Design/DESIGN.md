## Overview

Nestovin is a premium, trustworthy, community-first home-services brand. The product should feel calm, human, professional, modern and easy to understand. V1 services are **Maid, Cook and Caretaker only**. The product is a company-operated service model, not an open marketplace. The same visual identity applies across the Resident App, Employee App, Unified Admin Portal and informational Public Website.

Preserve the supplied Nestovin logo and brand identity. Use Manrope for headings and display; use Inter for body and interface content. Prefer clean white and mint surfaces, clear hierarchy, purposeful spacing, restrained teal/green accents, consistent line icons and warm, natural photography. Premium quality comes from clarity and consistency, not decorative effects.

## Colors

The fixed brand palette is Primary Teal `#00897B`, Primary Green `#43A047`, Background Mint `#F5FAF9` and Charcoal `#111827`. Neutral and semantic colours are represented in the tokens above. The Brand Kit values are fixed; UI action variants are supporting interface colours and do not alter the brand identity.

Use white and mint for most surfaces. Use teal as the principal brand signal and green as a supporting accent. Use charcoal/neutral text for readability. Semantic colours indicate status only and must never be the sole signal of meaning.

The brand teal `#00897B` and green `#43A047` do not meet 4.5:1 contrast with white for normal-sized text. Use `teal-action` `#00796B` for primary white-on-colour controls and `green-action` `#2E7D32` only when a green filled action is genuinely needed. These accessibility-oriented interface variants do not replace the approved brand palette. Recheck each actual foreground/background pair.

## Typography

Manrope is the primary typeface for headings and display; Inter is the secondary typeface for body, forms, labels, tables and dense interface content. Follow the typography tokens. The Brand Kit desktop H1 is 56/64 px; use the mobile title tokens on narrow screens. Do not use caption-size text for essential payment, service, deadline or legal information. Prefer weights 400, 500, 600 and 700.

## Layout

Use a 4 px base spacing rhythm. The standard spacing tokens run from 4 px through 64 px. Start mobile design from a 390 px reference width and verify at 360 px. Respect safe areas and keyboard insets. Start with 20 px mobile content margins. Use a stable responsive grid for web; keep Admin Portal tables information-dense only where operationally useful and always readable.

Keep one dominant primary action per screen. Use progressive disclosure for task coverage, plan conditions and secondary information. Reflow content instead of compressing type or controls. Use familiar mobile conventions and consistent navigation.

## Elevation & Depth

Prefer surface contrast, whitespace and subtle borders over heavy shadows. Use low-opacity elevation only for genuinely floating controls, anchored menus or modal/dialog surfaces. Avoid excessive card nesting, glassmorphism, coloured glow and shadows on every surface. Use spacing and grouping before containers.

## Shapes

Use the tokenised 8, 12, 16 and 24 px radius scale consistently. Use pill shapes for status chips and circular avatar containers. Keep geometry coherent; do not mix sharp and excessively rounded styles on the same screen. Use the approved geometric brand pattern sparingly in onboarding and marketing surfaces, not behind dense transactional content.

## Components

Primary buttons use the accessible teal-action fill with white text and a minimum 48 dp mobile target. Secondary buttons use a light surface with teal-action border/text. Tertiary actions are text-led. Destructive actions use explicit wording and error styling. Buttons require default, hover/focus (web), pressed, disabled and loading states.

Inputs use persistent labels, helper text and field-level errors; placeholders are examples, not labels. Starting input height is 52 px. Preserve entered values after recoverable errors. Cards group meaningful information and contain concise titles and a clear action. Status indicators combine semantic colour with a label and, where useful, an icon. Use tables/lists for repetitive operational data instead of a card for every value.

Resident App navigation should be resident-oriented and low-friction. Employee App should prioritise today's assignment, schedule, attendance and completion. Admin Portal is one unified application, filtered by Super Admin-assigned module/action permissions and the user's data scope. The Public Website is informational and enquiry-led; do not introduce online booking.

## Iconography

Use one clean, consistent line-icon family. Follow the Brand Kit's service icon direction for Maid, Cook and Caretaker and its home/community icon style. Proposed defaults are 24 px standard icon box, 20 px compact icon box and consistent 1.75–2 px stroke. Pair unfamiliar icons with text labels. Do not mix outline, filled, 3D, emoji and unrelated icon styles. Do not introduce symbols for services outside V1.

## Imagery

Use warm natural daylight, believable homes and residential communities, and respectful professional worker imagery. Use imagery to establish trust or explain context, not to fill empty space. Avoid exaggerated luxury environments and generic stock-photo styling. Keep image ratios, crops and corner treatments consistent.

## Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Maintain at least 4.5:1 contrast for normal text, 3:1 for large text and required non-text UI contrast. Do not use colour alone to convey status. Use 48 × 48 dp as the mobile touch-target design baseline. Provide visible keyboard focus, persistent labels, actionable field errors, accessible names, logical headings, text resizing and reduced-motion support.

## Nestovin Product Guardrails

- V1 services are Maid, Cook and Caretaker only.
- Nestovin manages its own workforce; this is not an open marketplace.
- Residents do not choose a named employee.
- Demand-led initial payment percentage is configured by Super Admin per demand cycle and locked when that cycle is published. Never hard-code 30%/70%; display the actual configured percentage and outstanding amount from the applicable cycle/booking snapshot.
- Continuous-mode bookings require 100% payment and capacity validation before confirmation.
- Do not present a demand request as a confirmed booking or active service.
- Show only Admin-published slots backed by feasible capacity; do not promise 24×7 availability in V1.
- Routine employee assignments do not require employee accept/reject; optional additional-work/overtime offers do.
- Attendance uses OTP or QR with event-based location evidence; do not design continuous employee live tracking.
- Admin module permission and cluster data scope are separate checks.
- Public Website is informational and enquiry-led; no online service booking.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do preserve the approved logo proportions, colours, lockups and clear space.
- Do use only the three approved V1 service categories.
- Do make demand-led and continuous experiences clearly distinguishable.
- Do display configured payment values dynamically from the applicable cycle/booking snapshot.
- Do show payment and service states only after authoritative backend confirmation.
- Do maintain readable contrast, visible labels, adequate touch targets and responsive layouts.
- Do design loading, empty, pending, error, disabled, restricted and success states.
- Don't invent plan names, prices, entitlements, task coverage, dates, legal terms or policies.
- Don't present a demand request as instant/confirmed service.
- Don't use brand teal/green as small white-on-colour text fills without checking contrast.
- Don't communicate state through colour alone.
- Don't add excessive shadows, gradients, glassmorphism, decorative animation or nested cards.
- Don't redesign, stretch, rotate, recolour or add effects to the logo.
- Don't add open-marketplace browsing, live employee tracking, deferred Admin placeholders or online website booking.
